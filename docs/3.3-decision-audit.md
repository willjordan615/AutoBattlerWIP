## Static conflict hypotheses (not measured effect sizes)

- Target score does not check visibility or immediate access. Action can prefer an inaccessible target over an accessible alternative; measure frequency and correlate with releases rather than assuming all such decisions are bad.
- Distance and safety are exact opposites in target choice; kill and challenger are complements. Kill and damage both favor low health, with the damage term potentially dominating at very low HP. Ability is target-invariant.
- Threat rewards selection of high damage or self-focused targets but penalizes exposure in position scoring. The same weight can pull target preference and movement in opposing directions.
- Target isolation measures separation from the candidate's teammates; position isolation measures other opponents near the proposed point, including other teams. Protection also changes meaning. Target preferredRange centers on full reach; position on 85%.
- A focus bonus .2 and switch threshold .24/.6 combine with Restless avoidance. Target selection runs in intent and action passes; intent snapshots are not fully simultaneous because focus is written as each fighter is visited. Do not remove either pass without paired evidence.
- Holding adds up to .35 in-range plus approach terms and Patience; free movement only considers a one-tick step. Route/support/escape commitments use different score precedence. Cooperative support can move toward a different enemy without selecting it; existing attack access guard checks any enemy, whereas attack initiation checks chosen target only.
- Ranged safety, strong range fit and readiness/access incentives may compete. Static inspection does not establish causality for ranged underperformance.
- Threat/safety aggregate exposure shares a term; both may scale with enemy count. Formation and yielding are additional terms, not represented by the ten base weights. Preserve formation as a legitimate strategy.

## Diagnostics

Opt in with battle.decisionAudit={interval:15,limit:128,units:{}}; no normal-state allocation. Target trace captures selected and highest-scoring alternative with feature and contribution maps, raw winner, taunt or hysteresis, signed margin; stage-specific call counts, action switches and intent/action disagreements. Movement trace counts action frames, selected/alternate access, idle and hold without access, sampled range ratio and nearest ally. Free-candidate trace captures exact final candidate score, pressure, progress and hold bonus for winner and runner-up; not a position-feature decomposition. Route/support/escape mode labels are inferred from post-selection flags; they are descriptive, not exact branch IDs. Samples bounded per fighter, sample interval default half a second; short-lived events can be missed. Stun frames skipped by action pass are not in action-frame denominators. Compare alongside existing navigation diagnostics; a timeout or a hold is not itself a failure.

Run: node scripts/fighter-decision-audit.js 2 1 1 --json (2 seeds x 3 arenas x 3 squad sizes = 18 paired fixture rounds, plus one paired strategic full match). Each observation-on round is compared against an observation-off round at identical seeds and input, every 30 ticks and final state; compare ranking and full match state. RNG function is omitted by JSON serialization; compare downstream state, not closure state. JSON includes per-unit samples and separate navigation counts. Aggregate rates by chassis using actionFrames or actionCalls as appropriate; don't pool fixture and full-match results for causal claims. To shard use disjoint seed ranges and report actual completed counts. No thresholds tuned yet.
