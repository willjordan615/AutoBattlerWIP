# Client performance
Options stores a validated preset and reduced-motion preference locally; default Balanced, honors OS reduced-motion on first use. No hardware profile or new network telemetry. Targets: Saver 20 canvas FPS, 540×390 raster, no floating damage/afterimages/grid/wall sparks, 60-effect cap; Balanced 30 FPS, 720×520, 120-effect cap; High 60 FPS, 720×520, 220-effect cap. Keep fighter labels/HP, team colors, targeting and core attack, projectile and hazard cues. Reduced motion disables afterimages and floating damage separately. Simulation remains fixed at 30 Hz; client clock uses elapsed time, not number of draws. Solo suspends when hidden and resumes without catch-up; Quick Play continues server-side, client catches up or consumes authoritative state on return. Paused Solo and completed replays stop scheduling frames. Saver schedules fewer callbacks as well as draws. Advisor samples only local normal-speed rendering; two consecutive slow windows recommend a lower preset, never change settings without selection. Targets are not guarantees; measure battery, FPS and readability on real low-end Android before tuning. Audio settings deferred until audio assets exist.

Screen wake lock: enabled by default, independent of graphics preset. Uses the browser's native screen lock while foreground matchmaking, drafting, unpaused combat or inter-round results are visible. Releases on pause, menu, final result, page hide or backgrounding; requests again on returning to an eligible phase. If denied or revoked by the OS, normal sleep applies; a later user interaction may retry no more frequently than every 30 seconds. No timer heartbeat, silent media, or network calls. Unsupported browsers retain normal sleep behavior. Keeping the display on costs battery even in Saver.

Options groups Graphics & accessibility, Display & battery, Match history & saved data, and Privacy & playtest statistics. Audio controls will join this screen when implemented. Resume Solo stays on the launch menu; discard is in saved data. Existing preferences and consent are unchanged.

Visual theme is independent of graphics quality. Scribble tabletop is the experimental default; Classic restores original canvas rendering. Missing art falls back to Classic. Three original Kenney PNGs are bundled locally; no remote image requests. Terrain is cached by arena and grid setting. Both themes retain logical bounds, team indicators, class labels and health bars. See assets/scribble/CREDITS.md.
