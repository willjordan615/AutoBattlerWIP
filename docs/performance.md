# Client performance
Options stores a validated preset and reduced-motion preference locally; default Balanced, honors OS reduced-motion on first use. No hardware profile or new network telemetry. Targets: Saver 20 canvas FPS, 540×390 raster, no floating damage/afterimages/grid/wall sparks, 60-effect cap; Balanced 30 FPS, 720×520, 120-effect cap; High 60 FPS, 720×520, 220-effect cap. Keep fighter labels/HP, team colors, targeting and core attack, projectile and hazard cues. Reduced motion disables afterimages and floating damage separately. Simulation remains fixed at 30 Hz; client clock uses elapsed time, not number of draws. Solo suspends when hidden and resumes without catch-up; Quick Play continues server-side, client catches up or consumes authoritative state on return. Paused Solo and completed replays stop scheduling frames. Saver schedules fewer callbacks as well as draws. Advisor samples only local normal-speed rendering; two consecutive slow windows recommend a lower preset, never change settings without selection. Targets are not guarantees; measure battery, FPS and readability on real low-end Android before tuning. Audio settings deferred until audio assets exist.
