# 3.3 controlled personality testing

The position/engagement matrix (45843797724) completed 946 rounds with 198 regression tests passed. Combined medium improved engagement availability, especially ranged, without high-dose damage shifts. Position-only and safety-pressure remain useful references. We need to measure player agency before choosing production heuristics.

## Design
Compare control, position-only, position+medium engagement, and position+safety pressure. Four new seeds (5-8), all three arenas, all three focal chassis, two opposite spawn corners per seed, full and 30% starting health. All four teams have identical two-chassis compositions (focal chassis plus a fixed companion, rotated by chassis). All four first-slot units share the health stratum. Only one focal unit's weights differ from the matched baseline; all remaining units, positions, RNG seed, modules and stats are identical. Opposite corners are balanced across seeds. This is an intervention on a single fighter, not a complete match or a team-wide draft strategy.

12 profiles: default, safety zero, safety/damage/isolation +1 and +3 via existing card deltas, threat +1 and +3 as direct diagnostic probes, protection +2, preferred range +2. Threat has no current draft card; zero safety is also a diagnostic probe, not an offered card. No new draft content or changes to module balance. No assumption of an optimal personality.

Per condition/chassis: 4 seeds x 3 arenas x 2 corners x 2 health strata = 48 fixture strata, 576 simulations including reused baseline, 528 paired comparisons. Total 6,912 simulations (including 576 distinct baseline simulations), 6,336 paired comparisons. 24 parallel jobs; 288 simulations per job. Same seeds across arms. Report paired mean deltas, direction counts, eligible denominators, and separate full/low health. Raw reports retain each pair.

## Measures
Safety: exposure fraction, low-health exposure, safe access, survival, HP. Threat: selection of a visible in-range opponent focused on the focal unit, conditional on such an opponent existing; not proof of intent. Damage: missed accessible alternatives, releases per second alive, damage. Isolation: selected opponent distance to its own teammates when any exist. Protection: selection of opponents focused on living allies, conditional on such opponents existing; support and ally distance. Preferred range: absolute range error relative to .85 of nominal range. Additional outcome measures include placement, holding, target changes, timeouts, and pursuit stalls. Post-tick samples for geometry; action telemetry for access/hold/switching. Report null where denominator absent; never count it as zero. Survival/time alive may affect exposure and damage; do not infer cause solely from aggregate observations.

Run node scripts/fighter-personalities.js <condition> <chassis> <start seed> <count>. No production code edits. Do not adopt coefficients solely by win rate: assess dose response, direction consistency, trade-offs, cross-chassis differences and preference clarity. Follow with actual drafted combinations if the single-weight interventions are legible.
