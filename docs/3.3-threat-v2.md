# 3.3 threat evaluation follow-up

The previous threat arm changed both consumers and multiplied pressure by nominal damage. It was unfavorable to ranged fighters (1/96 survivors in default fixtures vs 19/96 control). Preserve it as a historical comparison, not a candidate for adoption.

Threat v2 means current or near-future access to engagement by an opponent. It is not damage severity. Current clear terrain line is necessary. Present and forecast reach use the opponent's actual center-distance range, not collision padding. A present in-range opponent scores .6 to 1 based on depth within reach. Forecast uses the existing .45-second movement intent, requires clear terrain at the forecast point and along forecast travel, and contributes 25% vs 75% current. Focus on the evaluating actor multiplies by 1 vs .75 otherwise. No damage, health, or cooldown scaling in this feature. The exact coefficients are experimental; focus is a proxy, not proof of future behavior. The reach boundary is discontinuous by design for this first test; monitor oscillation.

Keep target and position weights unchanged; positive target threat favors attending to pressure, positive position threat discourages aggregate pressure (existing .5 scaling retained). Safety remains the legacy feature including its separate damage-weighted exposure term, so the experiment does not isolate all damage-based risk in the AI. No other AI or combat changes. Existing safety/engagement arms remain available in CLI but are not in this matrix.

Five conditions: control, original threat, v2 both, v2 target only, v2 position only. Two seed shards [1,2] and [3,4], each 36 fixture rounds and two full matches. Total planned 360 fixture rounds and 20 full matches. Compare matched default and preference fixtures separately; full matches are contextual, not matched roster comparisons after divergence. Add immediate visible opponent access and focus-aligned selection counts (post-tick, living units only); rates conditioned on respective pressure frames. Existing metrics retained. No compulsory targeting or movement, and no player-programming change.

Run: node scripts/fighter-evaluations.js threat-v2 1 2; workflow automatically runs branch matrix. Review counts, chassis damage and survival, access, focused pressure recognition, target switches, timeout, and recoveries; compare against original threat and consumer ablations before recommending any promotion. No production change.
