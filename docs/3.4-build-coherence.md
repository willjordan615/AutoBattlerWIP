## Implementation

New pure draft-coherence.js projects every legal card/slot through actual assign on a private player copy. Compare before/after graph potential for complementary active modules, including across teammates. Active utility slots, actual target-weight deltas, mutually exclusive morphology and bounded stack effects all matter. Adds capped +/-8 points (0.75 × marginal graph value) to the prior scorer, not a reward for total existing investment. Individual examples: mobility and Momentum, slow and Hazardous, Precision and Challenger, range and range preferences, support and recipient cooldowns, ally follow-up, protect and screen. These are mechanical relationships and hypotheses; not fixed build recipes or guarantees. Cross-unit effects require another equipped teammate; passive replacements can lose existing links. Personality remains the prior continuous profile, with no automatic lock-in. No additional RNG draws; no combat rollout. Legacy and candidate (first-pass profile) scorers remain explicit controls; coherent is default on this branch.

## Validation

Compare score-graph gain when positive opportunities are available, lost links, final graph potential, recruitment, replacement, pairwise diversity, placements and round timeouts. Actual damage credit and equipped unit-round exposures for selected effects offer a separate behavioral check (not causal synergy or efficacy estimates, and Expose credit can go to another fighter). Two scripted player-harness games (4501,4502), actual picks and traces. All-legacy/all-candidate group names in the reusable harness describe control/challenger allocation, not the actual policy identities; consult control/challenger keys. No post-merge deployment until results reviewed.

Graph measurement is endogenous to the scorer, so higher graph potential alone does not prove stronger builds. Assess whether realized effects and competitive results justify retention, and avoid over-rewarding duplicate or redundant effects. No runtime dependency. Main stays 3.3.
