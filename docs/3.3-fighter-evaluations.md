Player programming through draftable targeting preferences is a core design invariant. Do not remove behavioral drafting; later consider small composable portfolios rather than mandatory profiles. ## Isolated conditions
- control: untouched feature formulas.
- safety: only safety feature is multiplied by 1 + missing health fraction (1 at full health, 2 at zero); existing weight still controls influence. No mandatory retreat or threshold.
- engagement: target damage feature receives +.65 for direct access, or up to +.20 for nearby visible targets beyond reach; no bonus behind terrain. Keep original damage feature and positional scoring. This is a soft preference and can lose to other weights.
- threat: target threat and position threat share a bounded per-opponent pressure measure based on distance, visibility, short existing intent forecast, nominal damage and focus. Target threat favors attention to local pressure; position threat remains a penalty. Existing safety formula is unchanged in this arm. No new metric, reaction, or override.

Values are provisional experimental choices, not final balance. Do not combine arms before comparing.
