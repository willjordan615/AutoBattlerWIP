# 3.3 hybrid preference audit

Decision after personality matrix 45845504503: retain safety and preferred-range semantics; use revised positional threat as leading candidate, compare with combined .40 engagement. Personality sweep established substantial safety response but inconsistent Aggressor behavior, small Duelist effects and insufficient Guardian opportunities. It did not measure draft competitiveness. Do not infer weak card value from weak observed behavioral separation alone; Guardian x3 was favorable in the older 3.2 audit.

User authorizes updates and testing, including potential statistical benefits alongside weight changes. Before changing production card balance, evaluate the following provisional hybrid choices with existing raw-stat modifiers: Aggressor +1 damage (half a raw damage increment); Duelist +4.5 movement speed (quarter of raw speed increment); Guardian +10 HP (half a raw HP increment). Use HP rather than a new defense mechanic to avoid changes to engine rules. These are diagnostic hypotheses, not agreed card values. No production cards or catalog change. Raw-stat increments are synthetic benchmarks; the catalog has no separate raw-stat cards.

Each focal card tested at one and three picks, matched no-card, weight-only, small-stat-only, hybrid and full-stat-only. Same initial seed and all non-focal units. Reuse baseline across doses. Two AI configurations, all chassis and arenas, four new seeds 9-12, two opposite focal starting corners, mirror and mixed teams (focal pair, one singleton, one triple, one other pair). Full starting health; a hybrid HP bonus changes max and initial HP intentionally. No complete draft matches or contested picks in this pass. Mixed layouts create more variation for isolation/protection; singleton selection when both singleton and grouped opponents exist is recorded separately from distance to teammates. Stats-only comparison disambiguates whether benefit is entirely statistical, behavioral preference is retained and stacking has disproportionate effects.

36 jobs, 24 strata/job, 9 actual simulations/stratum, 7 contrasts per dose (14 paired contrasts/stratum): 7,776 rounds, 12,096 paired contrasts (reused observations, not independent rounds). Summary includes denominator and direction counts; raw artifacts retain all metrics. Compare rank/survival/HP, exposure, safe access, missed access, releases, isolation and cooperative selection; use per-layout results for relevant metrics. Do not choose a bonus solely by higher damage or win rate. If a hybrid cannot outperform or meaningfully differ from the same small stat alone, improve the weight's semantic definition before adopting. If hybrid dominates full-stat reference across chassis/doses, reduce bonus. Strong distinct situational effects acceptable. Follow with actual draft evaluation after choosing candidates.
