# Quiet tabletop audio
Native Web Audio consumer, independent of simulation. Ten local Kenney CC0 Ogg clips: heavy metal (Bruiser), cloth (Rogue), pluck (Ranged), soft impact, metallic guard, glass utility, wood click, select confirmation, bong round start, confirmation finish. See assets/audio/CREDITS.md. No music, footsteps, DOT tick sounds or continuous loops. Sound defaults enabled at Combat 30% / Interface 25%, further attenuated by master .62 and per-cue gains. One prioritized cue per engine tick, per-group cooldowns, 160ms minimum combat spacing, max four voices, quiet stereo positioning and bounded .94–1.06 pitch variation. Short clipped envelopes fade out at .28s combat/UI or .65s transitions. Audio starts after trusted click, pointer-up or key gesture; retries context resume without sharing stale pending promises. If preview cannot immediately use decoded Web Audio, play through native HTML audio directly inside the activation and retain the native fallback. Both backends share bounded voice counts and event cooldown policy. The Options preview reports playback or errors. Clips load once, stops/suspends when hidden/muted, ignores paused stepping, sped-up replays and Quick Play catch-up. Unsupported or missing audio is silent and never blocks the game. Preview and local preferences in Options; no engine RNG or network telemetry. Validate audible balance and supported browser decoding on devices.
