# Quiet tabletop audio
Native Web Audio consumer, independent of simulation. Ten local Kenney CC0 Ogg clips: heavy metal (Bruiser), cloth (Rogue), pluck (Ranged), soft impact, metallic guard, glass utility, wood click, select confirmation, bong round start, confirmation finish. See assets/audio/CREDITS.md. No music, footsteps, DOT tick sounds or continuous loops. Sound defaults enabled at Combat 30% / Interface 25%, further attenuated by master .32 and per-cue gains. One prioritized cue per engine tick, per-group cooldowns, 160ms minimum combat spacing, max four voices, quiet stereo positioning and bounded .94–1.06 pitch variation. Short clipped envelopes fade out at .28s combat/UI or .65s transitions. Audio starts after user gesture, loads once, stops/suspends when hidden/muted, ignores paused stepping, sped-up replays and Quick Play catch-up. Unsupported or missing audio is silent and never blocks the game. Preview and local preferences in Options; no engine RNG or network telemetry. Validate audible balance and supported browser decoding on devices.
