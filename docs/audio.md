# Quiet tabletop audio
Original offline-generated procedural audio: node scripts/generate-audio.js. Twelve mono 22.05kHz 16-bit PCM WAVs, seeded noise, damped resonances and bounded envelopes; no external samples or music. No synthesis work in gameplay. Distinct dense granular low-end Bruiser, broad noise-swept Rogue, string-like ranged, hollow airy Hazardous variant, impact, guard, utility, wood-like click/confirmation, restrained start/end tones. Semantic attackRelease events carry source identity, chassis and Hazardous flag. Select one variant per attack, not stacked overlay sounds; extend modifier mapping as useful. Audio no longer follows attackCommit during windup. The native Web Audio consumer and HTML media fallback retain default Combat 30% / Interface 25%, master .62 and per-cue attenuation, up to six voices, two distinct prioritized groups per tick, per-group cooldowns, three combat starts per rolling 250 ms per backend, per-playback variation on both backends (Bruiser ±10%, Rogue ±12%, other combat ±7.5% pitch/rate; combat gain 82–100% of nominal), stable UI tones (±2% pitch, 96–100% gain), and stereo placement where supported. Audio pauses in background and ignores paused steps, sped-up replay and online catch-up. Options includes per-cue audition selector. No music stings, continuous footsteps or DOT sounds; no engine RNG or rules changes. Playback activation and native fallback unchanged. Validate audible quality on devices.

Melee releases are classified after evasion filtering: successful contact uses the chassis sound; no landed contact uses the quiet air miss. Projectile launch retains its cue; a whole volley with no landed contact emits one miss on its final expiration (including wall absorption). Partial volley hits do not emit false misses. Raw projectile collision sounds are ignored; damage cues follow resolved damage. Rogue cue gain .32, down from .60; miss gain .20. No simulation rules or RNG changes.

Background music uses a single lazy-loaded, locally hosted Ogg loop, The Old Tower Inn by RandomMind (CC0). Default volume 12%, independent music enable/volume preferences, global sound toggle applies to both music and effects. Starts on trusted gesture, pauses when hidden or muted, resumes at the same position, and does not restart on round changes. Separate from the six SFX voices. No remote streams or new runtime dependencies. Future custom soundtrack can replace the local music file with compatible licensed audio and updated credits.
